## 1. The Thesis: Trust as a Service (TaaS)
In the current Web3 landscape, trust is fragmented. Liquidity on Ethereum doesn't know about reputation on Stellar. Real-world solvency (Web2 data) is invisible to DeFi. This "Trust Gap" forces protocols to require massive over-collateralization and limits the autonomy of AI agents.

**Zolvency solves this by becoming the Universal Identity Oracle.**

## 2. The Golden Triangle: Stellar + ZK + Axelar

Our architecture leverages three distinct technological pillars to create a "Moat" of efficiency and privacy:

### ⚡ Stellar (The Identity Factory)
We use Stellar (Soroban) as our core registry because it offers the lowest cost per verification for complex cryptography. By verifying ZK proofs on Stellar, we reduce the cost of trust by 100x compared to Ethereum Mainnet.

### 🛡️ Zero-Knowledge (The Privacy Compressor)
Data privacy is non-negotiable. ZK proofs (via Reclaim) allow users to prove their GitHub contributions, bank statements, or Uber ratings without revealing the underlying data. This "compresses" gigabytes of private data into a single, immutable "Stamp of Approval."

### 🌐 Axelar (The Global Messenger)
Interoperability is our multiplier. Axelar allows us to export the trust generated on Stellar to any chain (Ethereum, Arbitrum, Solana, etc.). We don't compete with other chains; we empower them with verified reputation.

### 💰 x402 (The Economic Engine)
Trust without liquidity is static. By integrating the **x402 protocol**, we enable agents to pay for their own infrastructure. x402 activates the HTTP 402 "Payment Required" status code, allowing APIs and agents to pay for reputation atests and cross-chain messaging instantly and programmatically using XLM or stablecoins.

## 3. The Killer App: The Agentic Economy
The next wave of Web3 users won't be humans; they will be **AI Agents**.

AI Agents need to:
- Invest in DeFi.
- Pay for compute resources.

**But why should a contract trust an agent?**
Zolvency provides the **Agentic Proof of Authority (PoA)**. A human (owner of a Soul ID) can delegate specific powers to an agent. This delegation is verified on Stellar and presented on any other chain via Axelar.

### The "Light Passport" Flow:
1. **Human creates a Soul ID** on Stellar (linked to Web2 reputation via ZK).
2. **Human authorizes an Agent** for a specific task (e.g., "Invest 100 USDC in Uniswap").
3. **Zolvency exports this authority** via Axelar.
4. **Target contract on Ethereum** verifies the message and allows the Agent to execute, trusting the "Seal of Approval" from the Zolvency Hub.
