# Zolvency Product Strategy: The Programmable Trust Layer

## 1. Executive Summary
Zolvency is a decentralized infrastructure designed to bridge Web2 private data into Web3 on-chain reputation. By leveraging **zkTLS (Reclaim Protocol)**, **Soroban Host Functions**, and **Cross-Chain Interoperability**, Zolvency creates a portable, private, and verifiable identity system for humans and AI agents.

## 2. The Core Innovation: Dual-Token Architecture
Unlike monolithic identity systems, Zolvency separates *being* from *proving*:

| Component | Role | Persistence | Data Sensitivity |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Soul ID (Hub)** | Identity Anchor | Permanent | Anonymous |
| **ZK Tokens (Spoke)** | Attribute Proofs | Dynamic / TTL | ZK-Hidden |

### Why it's Revolutionary:
It solves the **Identity Trilemma**:
1. **Privacy:** User data never leaves their device; only ZK proofs reach the chain.
2. **Verifiability:** Smart contracts autonomously verify proofs using native host functions.
3. **Portability:** Reputation earned on Stellar can be exported to any chain via Axelar/LayerZero.

## 3. Product Pillars & Business Models

### A. Reputation-as-a-Service (RaaS)
- **Problem:** dApps can't trust users without excessive collateral.
- **Solution:** Zolvency provides a "Trust Score" API.
- **Revenue:** Query fees paid in XLM by third-party protocols.

### B. Credit-as-a-Service (CaaS)
- **Problem:** DeFi is over-collateralized.
- **Solution:** ZK proofs of off-chain bank balances and income.
- **Revenue:** Spread sharing with lending protocols.

### C. Agentic Trust (Identity for AI)
- **Problem:** AI agents lack economic identity and authority bounds.
- **Solution:** Delegated Soul IDs with ZK-enforced spend/policy limits.
- **Revenue:** Enterprise SaaS for AI safety and compliance.

## 4. Strategic Advantage on Stellar (Soroban)
- **Performance:** Native Ed25519 verification is 100x cheaper than EVM-based ZK verification.
- **Anchor Network:** Direct connection to real-world financial rails.
- **Interoperability:** Built-in support for cross-chain messaging makes Zolvency a "Global Trust Bridge".
